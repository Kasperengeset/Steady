# Health branch: activity features on Depresjon

Generated by `ml/health/train.py` on 2026-11-14. 1029 full days from 55 people (23 depressed, 32 controls). Features from `backend/app/pipeline/health.py`, the same code the app runs on Fitbit data.

Leave-one-patient-out (asserted). A person's score is the mean of their day probabilities, cut-off 0.5.

## Per feature

Standardised beta from logistic regression on all days. Within-person d = (mean of depressed people − mean of controls) / within-person SD: how far a full depressed-vs-control difference moves a feature compared with its normal day-to-day swing. Direction is the literature direction the engine uses.

| Feature | Direction | Beta | Within-person d | Engine weight |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| steps | lower = worse | -0.70 | -0.40 | 0.143 |
| inactive_share | higher = worse | -0.08 | +0.26 | 0.143 |
| night_activity | higher = worse | +0.12 | -0.00 | 0.000 |
| relative_amplitude | lower = worse | +0.44 | +0.23 | 0.000 |
| m10_onset | higher = worse | +0.59 | +0.45 | 0.143 |
| sleep_hours | lower = worse | (no data) | (no data) | 0.143 |
| sleep_midpoint | higher = worse | (no data) | (no data) | 0.143 |
| resting_hr | higher = worse | (no data) | (no data) | 0.143 |
| hrv_rmssd | lower = worse | (no data) | (no data) | 0.143 |

Betas that disagree with the literature: inactive_share, relative_amplitude. A beta can flip because the features overlap (B6), so the engine judges each feature by its own group difference (d): equal weight if d moves in the literature direction, weight 0 if not or if |d| < 0.1 (night_activity, relative_amplitude). Zero-weight features are still computed and can be shown to the clinician.

## Limitations

- Actiwatch counts are not Fitbit steps. The model shows the patterns exist; in the app the live signal is the per-person z-score, which removes the scale.
- Between-person classification, used as evidence for within-person change (same caveat as acoustic).
- 55 people, 14 to 45 days each. The condition group includes bipolar patients (unipolar-only row above).
- Sleep, resting heart rate and HRV have no training data here; their directions come from RADAR-MDD and related literature.

## Real Fitbit data (Google Health API)

One team member, 30 consecutive days from a Fitbit, pulled with `scripts/google_health_sync.py` and replayed through the engine: the first 14 days built the healthy baseline, the next 17 were scored. The daily score S stayed between −2.3 and +0.8 and never crossed 1, so no false alarm on ordinary days. One person and 17 days: a sanity check, not a false-alarm rate. Raw data is not in the repo.
