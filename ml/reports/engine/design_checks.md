## 3. Engagement as a signal (#27)

Noise ×1.5, one prompted check-in a day done with probability 0.85 − drop × d (d = latent depression, 0.1 when well, ~0.85 in a full relapse), minus 0.4 during busy spells (~5 days, starting 1/30 days). Engagement D = (baseline rate − done today) / √(p(1−p)), SD 1 when nothing changes; it is combined with the acoustic anchored D as (D_ac + w·D_eng)/√(1+w²), and alone on a missed day. Weight 0 = today's engine (a missed day has no score).
